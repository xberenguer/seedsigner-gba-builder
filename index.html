FROM FILE TO FIRST PLAY

Three steps.
One little device.

  1. 01

    Make it yours.

    Choose your GBA game and build your image. The download includes the game, its controls and SeedSigner.

  2. 02

    Flash. Power. Play.

    In Raspberry Pi Imager, choose Use custom and write your IMG to an SD card. Insert it into your Pi and power on.

  3. 03

    Switch when ready.

    Hold the top + bottom side buttons for 2 seconds, then release all buttons to open SeedSigner. Reboot to return to the game.

At the controlsV-Rally 3+

Before you flash+

This build supports Pi Zero 1.3 with the SeedSigner Plus 320 × 240 display. Other displays and consoles are not supported. GBA game compatibility and speed vary. There is no audio or persistent game saving.

Images from this browser builder still need physical Pi testing. It is an experimental community fork, with no independent security audit. The game and SeedSigner share one operating system; the button combination is not authentication. Test with disposable seeds first.
